## A possible structure: 
- Our topic 
- Our target users 
- Our 1 overall objective 
- Our 3 specific objectives 
- Our 5 or more answerable research questions 

### Target users 
- University administrators and students deciding on whether to make AI a key feature of their study routine
### Overall objective 
- To investigate how university students' use of generative AI relates to their academic and well-being outcomes, in order to help institutions and students make decisions on AI usage. 
### 3 specific objectives 

| #   | Objective                                                                                                    | Factor                                | Mainly serves  |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| 1   | To compare students' academic outcomes across AI use approaches                                              | Use case, prompt skill, subscription  | Students       |
| 2   | To compare students' well-being outcomes across levels of AI use                                             | Weekly AI hours, perceived dependency | Students       |
| 3   | To compare students' academic and well-being outcomes across institutional AI policies and study backgrounds | Policy, major, year                   | Administrators |
