### Target users
| User | Decision they face | What our analysis gives them | Objectives |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Students** | Whether, and for which tasks, to make ChatGPT part of their study routine | How peers use ChatGPT, and how their use relates to perceived benefits and emotions | 1, 2 |
| **University administrators** | Whether to introduce, and how to communicate, a ChatGPT policy | How policy awareness relates to students' use of and concerns about ChatGPT | 3 |

### Overall objective 
To investigate how higher-education students use ChatGPT, how their patterns of use relate to their perceived academic benefits, emotional experience and institutional context, **and how these changed between 2023–24 and 2024–25**, in order to inform students' and institutions' decisions about AI in study.

### 3 specific objectives 
| # | Objective | Variables | Serves |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | To compare students' **perceived academic benefits** across levels of ChatGPT use, subscription types and study tasks | Q13 (ChatGPT user or not), Q14 (free or paid version), Q15 (overall ChatGPT use), Q18 (use by study task), Q24 (satisfaction), Q26–27 (perceived academic benefits), Q35a (self-rated study success) | Students | 
| 2 | To compare students' **emotional experiences** across levels of ChatGPT use and perceived study difficulty | Q15 (overall ChatGPT use), Q32 (emotions while using ChatGPT), Q33 (perceived study difficulty) | Students | 
| 3 | To compare students' **use of AI chatbots, concerns about ChatGPT and awareness of ChatGPT policies** across survey years and study backgrounds | Q13 (AI chatbots used), Q15 (overall ChatGPT use), Q18 (use by study task), Q20 (institutional policy awareness), Q22 (ethical concerns), Q10 (field of study), **year** (survey wave) | Administrators |

*Question numbers refer to the survey questionnaire (`data/questionnaire(2023-2024).pdf and questionnaire(2024-2025).pdf`).*
