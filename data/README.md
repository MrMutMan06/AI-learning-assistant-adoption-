## Why this dataset fits our topic
It records student AI use (e.g. weekly generative AI hours, paid subscriptions, perceived AI dependency) alongside academic and well-being measures such as GPA, skill retention and burnout risk level.
## Limitations
1. **No documented collection method:** the Kaggle page has no description of how, when or where the data was collected, who the students are, or which institutions took part.
2. **Collection period unknown:** the creator gives no dates, so we cannot state when the data was gathered.
3. **Possibly simulated data:** the uploader does not say whether the records are real survey data or synthetic. A clean, perfectly complete set of 50,000 records is a warning sign, so findings may not reflect real students.
4. **Correlation, not causation:** the data cannot show that AI use causes changes in grades, burnout or skill retention.
5. **Self-reported or derived measures:** variables like perceived dependency and burnout level may be subjective, and how they were measured is unknown.
6. **Generalization:** without information on country, sampling or institutions, we cannot claim results apply to any specific group of students.
